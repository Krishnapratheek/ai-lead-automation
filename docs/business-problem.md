# Business Problem

## Problem

Businesses receive leads through multiple channels such as websites,
WhatsApp, forms and other communication channels.

Manually processing these leads creates several problems:

- Slow response times
- Inconsistent lead qualification
- Missed high-value leads
- Manual CRM updates
- Manual follow-up
- Lack of structured lead information
- Difficulty routing leads to the correct next action

## Proposed Solution

Build an AI-powered lead automation platform that can:

1. Capture incoming leads
2. Normalize lead information
3. Store lead information
4. Use an LLM to qualify the lead
5. Assign a lead score
6. Identify lead intent
7. Determine the next action
8. Route the lead appropriately
9. Notify the relevant person or system
10. Eventually communicate through WhatsApp and other channels
